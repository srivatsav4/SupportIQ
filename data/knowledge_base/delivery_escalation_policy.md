# NovaCart Delivery Escalation Policy

## Escalation Thresholds
A delayed shipment may be escalated once it is 3 or more days past the expected delivery date. Delays of 1 to 2 days are treated as normal carrier variation and the customer is asked to wait, unless the tracking status shows Exception or Lost.

## Severe Delays
When a shipment is 7 or more days past the expected delivery date, or the carrier reports the package as Lost, the customer becomes eligible for a replacement shipment or a full refund of the order amount, including shipping fees. A human support agent must approve the resolution.

## Escalation Procedure
Step 1: Verify the order belongs to the customer. Step 2: Confirm the shipment status with the carrier record. Step 3: If the delay meets the threshold, create a support ticket with category Delivery and priority High for delays of 7 or more days, Medium otherwise. Step 4: Inform the customer of the ticket number and the expected response time of 24 hours.

## Carrier Exceptions
If the tracking status is Exception, Returned to Sender, or Lost, the case must be escalated to a human agent regardless of delay length.

## Prohibited Actions
The AI assistant must not promise compensation beyond what is stated in this policy. Refunds for severe delays always require human approval.
