# NovaCart Exchange Policy

## Exchange Eligibility
Items may be exchanged for a different size, color, or variant of the same product within the return window for the customer's membership tier. Exchanges for a different product are handled as a return followed by a new order.

## Exchange Process
The customer creates an exchange request. The replacement is shipped after the original item is scanned by the carrier on its return trip. For Gold and Platinum members, the replacement ships immediately and the original item must be returned within 10 days.

## Stock Availability
Exchanges depend on inventory. If the requested variant is out of stock, the customer may choose a refund or wait for restock. Support must not promise a restock date.

## Exceptions
Non-returnable items listed in the Return Policy cannot be exchanged. Defective items are exchanged without a fee.
