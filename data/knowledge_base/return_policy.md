# NovaCart Return Policy

## Return Window
Standard members may return eligible items within 30 days of delivery. Gold members have 45 days. Platinum members have 60 days. The window starts on the delivery date recorded by the carrier.

## Return Conditions
Items must be unused, in original packaging, and include all accessories and manuals. Items with signs of wear beyond inspection may be rejected or partially refunded.

## Non-Returnable Items
Gift cards, opened personal care and hygiene products, perishable goods, activated software licenses, and custom-made items cannot be returned unless they are defective or incorrect.

## Return Process
The customer creates a return request. NovaCart issues a prepaid return label within 1 business day. The customer has 7 days to hand the package to the carrier. Once the warehouse receives the item, inspection takes 2 to 3 business days, after which the refund process starts.

## Return Shipping Cost
Return shipping is free when the item is defective, damaged, or incorrect. For change-of-mind returns, a return shipping fee of $5.99 is deducted from the refund, except for Platinum members who return free of charge.

## Examples
A Standard member received an order on day 1 and requests a return on day 25. The request is eligible. A Standard member requesting a return on day 35 is not eligible, but a Gold member on day 35 is eligible.
