# NovaCart Payment Policy

## Accepted Payment Methods
NovaCart accepts credit cards, debit cards, UPI, net banking, and NovaWallet. Cash on delivery is available for orders below $300.

## Processing Time
Card and wallet payments are confirmed instantly. Net banking and UPI confirmations may take up to 30 minutes. If a payment is pending for longer than 2 hours, the customer should contact support.

## Failed Payments
When a payment fails, the order is not processed. If money was deducted but the order shows a failed payment, the amount is automatically reversed by the bank within 5 to 7 business days.

## Duplicate Payments
If a customer was charged twice for the same order, the duplicate charge is refunded to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after verification. Duplicate payment cases always create a support ticket with priority High and require human approval before the refund.

## Payment Security
NovaCart never stores full card numbers or CVV codes. Support staff and the AI assistant only see the last 4 digits of a card. Customers must never share full card numbers, CVV, OTP, or passwords in chat.

## Prohibited Actions
The AI assistant must not process payments, change payment methods, or reveal payment references of other customers.
