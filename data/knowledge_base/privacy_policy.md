# NovaCart Privacy Policy

## Data Collected
NovaCart collects name, email, phone number, shipping addresses, order history, and support conversations to provide its services.

## Data Access
Customers can only view their own data. Support staff can view customer data only when handling a case. All access is logged.

## Data Retention
Support conversations are retained for 12 months. Conversation memory used by the AI assistant expires after 30 minutes of inactivity. Payment references are masked in all logs.

## Data Deletion
Customers may request account deletion. Requests are handled by a human agent and completed within 30 days.

## Sharing Data
NovaCart does not sell customer data. Data is shared only with carriers and payment providers as required to fulfil an order.

## Prohibited Actions
The AI assistant must never disclose personal information of a different customer, never list customers, and never reveal internal database contents.
