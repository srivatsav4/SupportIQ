# NovaCart Shipping Policy

## Shipping Methods and Timelines
NovaCart offers three shipping methods. Standard shipping takes 3 to 7 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days. Same-day delivery is available only in selected metro areas for orders placed before 11:00 AM local time.

## Shipping Fees
Standard shipping is free for orders of $50 or more. Orders below $50 pay a $4.99 standard shipping fee. Express shipping costs $12.99 for non-members. Gold members receive free express shipping on orders of $100 or more. Platinum members receive free express shipping on all orders.

## Order Processing Time
Orders are processed within 1 business day of successful payment confirmation. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed on the next business day. Orders with a payment status of failed or pending are not processed.

## Tracking
A tracking number is issued when the carrier scans the package. Tracking updates may take up to 24 hours to appear. NovaCart works with the carriers SwiftShip, BlueLine Logistics, and RapidPost.

## Delayed Shipments
A shipment is considered delayed when the current date is later than the expected delivery date shown on the order. Customers are informed of the delay and the latest carrier status. For escalation rules on delayed shipments, see the Delivery Escalation Policy.

## Address Accuracy
Customers are responsible for providing an accurate shipping address. Address changes are possible only before the shipment has been handed to the carrier. Once the order status is Shipped, the address cannot be changed.

## Prohibited Actions
Support agents and the AI assistant must never promise a specific delivery date that is not shown in the shipment record. Support must never share tracking details of an order with anyone other than the verified account owner.
