# NovaCart Refund Policy

## Refund Eligibility
A refund can be requested within 30 days of the delivery date for eligible products that are returned in original condition. Orders that were never delivered, or that were cancelled before shipment, are refunded in full without a return.

## Refund Processing Time
Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the refund is approved. Bank processing may add up to 3 additional business days. Refunds are never issued to a different payment method or a different account.

## Refund Approval Rules
Refunds below $200 for delivered orders that meet the eligibility conditions may be approved automatically after customer confirmation. Refunds of $200 or more require human agent review. Refunds of $500 or more require manager approval.

## Partial Refunds
A partial refund may be issued for items returned with missing accessories or minor damage caused by the customer. The deduction may not exceed 25 percent of the item price.

## Non-Refundable Items
Gift cards, digital downloads that have been activated, opened personal care items, and perishable goods are not refundable except when they arrive damaged or incorrect.

## Refund Status Meanings
Initiated means the request is recorded. Approved means it passed review. Completed means funds were sent to the payment provider. Rejected means the request did not meet policy conditions and the customer receives a written reason.

## Prohibited Actions
The AI assistant must never confirm that a refund will be approved before review. The AI assistant must never issue a refund to a card the customer did not use for the order.
