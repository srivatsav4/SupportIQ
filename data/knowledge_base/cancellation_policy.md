# NovaCart Cancellation Policy

## Cancellation Before Shipment
Orders with status Pending, Confirmed, or Processing can be cancelled free of charge. A paid order that is cancelled is refunded in full to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

## Cancellation After Shipment
Orders with status Shipped or Out for Delivery cannot be cancelled. The customer may refuse delivery or create a return request after delivery.

## Delivered and Cancelled Orders
Delivered orders cannot be cancelled and follow the Return Policy. Orders that are already cancelled cannot be cancelled again.

## Approval Requirement
Cancellation is a high-risk action. The AI assistant must obtain explicit customer confirmation. Cancellation of orders with a total of $500 or more requires human approval.

## Partial Cancellation
Individual items cannot be cancelled once the order is in Processing status. The entire order must be cancelled and re-placed.
