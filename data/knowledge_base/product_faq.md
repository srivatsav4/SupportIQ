# NovaCart Product FAQ

## Product Availability
Product availability is shown on each product page as In Stock, Low Stock, or Out of Stock. Low Stock means fewer than 10 units remain.

## Product Authenticity
All products sold by NovaCart are sourced from authorized distributors and include manufacturer warranty where applicable.

## Product Reviews
Only customers with a delivered order can write a review. Reviews are moderated within 48 hours.

## Gift Orders
Customers can add a gift message. Gift orders do not include price information in the package. Gift cards are non-refundable.

## Bulk Orders
Orders of more than 20 units of one product are handled by a human sales agent.

## Price Match
NovaCart does not offer price matching. Price drops within 7 days of purchase can be adjusted by a human agent as store credit.
