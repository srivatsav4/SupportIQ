# NovaCart Warranty Policy

## Warranty Periods
Warranty periods depend on the product category and are shown on the product page. Electronics carry 12 months. Home appliances carry 24 months. Accessories carry 6 months. Furniture carries 12 months. Clothing and consumables have no warranty beyond the damaged product policy.

## Warranty Start Date
The warranty starts on the delivery date of the order.

## What Is Covered
Manufacturing defects and hardware failures under normal use are covered. Covered claims are repaired or replaced at NovaCart's discretion.

## What Is Not Covered
Physical damage, liquid damage, unauthorized repairs, normal wear, cosmetic scratches, and loss or theft are not covered.

## Warranty Claim Process
The customer submits a claim with the order ID and a description of the issue. NovaCart replies within 2 business days. Approved claims are repaired or replaced within 10 business days.

## Extended Warranty
Platinum members receive 6 additional months of warranty on electronics.
