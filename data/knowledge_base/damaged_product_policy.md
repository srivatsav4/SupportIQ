# NovaCart Damaged Product Policy

## Reporting Damage
Damaged, defective, or incorrect items must be reported within 7 days of delivery. The customer should provide a description and clear photos of the item and packaging.

## Resolution Options
For confirmed damage, the customer may choose a free replacement or a full refund. Return shipping is free. Refunds follow the standard Refund Policy processing time.

## Wrong Product Delivered
If the product delivered is different from the product ordered, NovaCart sends the correct product at no cost and provides a free return label for the wrong item.

## Damage Reported After 7 Days
Claims after 7 days are reviewed by a human agent. Products under warranty are handled through the Warranty Policy instead.

## Fraud Checks
Repeated damage claims from the same account within 60 days are reviewed by a human agent before resolution. The AI assistant must not accuse a customer of fraud.

## Prohibited Actions
The AI assistant must not approve replacements or refunds for damage claims without a support ticket. Damage claims always create a support ticket with category Damaged Product.
