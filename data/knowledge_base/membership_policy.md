# NovaCart Membership Policy

## Membership Tiers
NovaCart has three tiers: Silver, Gold, and Platinum. Every new account starts at Silver.

## Tier Benefits
Silver: 30-day returns and standard support. Gold: 45-day returns, free express shipping on orders of $100 or more, and priority support. Platinum: 60-day returns, free express shipping on all orders, free return shipping, dedicated support, and 6 extra months of electronics warranty.

## Earning Tiers
Customers reach Gold after spending $1,000 within 12 months and Platinum after spending $3,000 within 12 months. Tier is reviewed on the first day of each month.

## Downgrades
A tier is downgraded when the spending threshold is not met in the trailing 12 months. Benefits of the previous tier remain valid for orders placed before the downgrade.
